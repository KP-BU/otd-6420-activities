OTD 6420 Mental Health Populations and Practice in OT

New interactive activity previews for faculty review

How to review: open each activity and try it as a student would. Then choose Use it, Don't use it, or Request a change under it, here or at the bottom of the activity, and add a note if you like. When you're done, select Copy my choices and paste them into an email to keith_pace@baylor.edu. Your choices are saved in this browser as you go. These previews don't record anything about how you do in the activities.
Module 1

Which determinant is at work?

Drag and Drop

Students sort ten client snapshots into four Healthy People 2030 social determinant of health domains.

Outcome: explain how social determinants of health impact clients (Ch. 28).

Open activity
Module 2

Patterns that shape the day

Drag the Words

Students complete eight sentences about habits, routines, roles, rituals and occupational balance.

Outcome: discuss the importance of performance patterns in a productive routine (OTPF-4, Ch. 14).

Open activity
Module 3

Name that approach

Dialog Cards

Seven scenarios; students name the OTPF-4 intervention approach (create/promote, establish/restore, maintain, modify, prevent).

Outcome: identify intervention strategies. Pairs with the remediation/compensation video.

Open activity
Module 4

Build a COAST goal

Dropdown

Students label the parts of two COAST goals for Ray and Lucy, then choose the most measurable wording for a third.

Outcome: develop client-centered goals based on the COAST method.

Open activity
Module 5

Settings across the continuum

Crossword

A 12-word crossword on practice settings: inpatient, partial hospitalization, clubhouse, forensic, ACT and more.

Outcome: hospital- vs community-based settings and the continuum of care (Ch. 35, 40–43).

Open activity
Module 6

Emerging practice: key terms

Flashcards

Eleven self-check cards on early intervention (Part C, IFSP), SAMHSA trauma-informed principles and consultation.

Outcomes: OT's role in early intervention and consultation (Ch. 5, 29, 36).

Open activity
Module 7

Sleep supports and sleep disruptors

Drag and Drop

Students sort ten everyday habits by whether they support or disrupt sleep.

Outcome: the importance of sleep and performance patterns for health and wellness (Ch. 52).
